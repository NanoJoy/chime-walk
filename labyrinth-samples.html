Backbite on hex cells

Starts as concentric rings walked in alternating directions, then loosened by backbite moves on the outer end while the centre end stays put. "Mix" is moves per cell: low keeps the big loops, high gives a winding space-filler. Corridors are one hex wide.

Maze-doubling on hex kites

Each hex is split into six kites. Walking around a random spanning tree of the hexes passes through every kite once, making a closed loop. The loop is cut at the centre, and a few backbite moves walk the loose end out to the rim. The tree style decides the character; "rings" prefers tree edges that run around the centre, which makes long circling corridors.
